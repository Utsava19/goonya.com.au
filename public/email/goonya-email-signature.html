Outlook Web — how to add this signature

  1. Open the yellow box for your name below (Utsav or Rakshya).
  2. Click inside it, then press Cmd+A (Mac) or Ctrl+A (Windows).
  3. Copy — Cmd+C / Ctrl+C.
  4. In Outlook Web: gear icon → View all Outlook settings.
  5. Go to Mail → Compose and reply → Email signature.
  6. Paste into the signature box and click Save.
  7. Send a test email to your phone to confirm layout.

Open this file in Chrome or Safari, then copy the rendered yellow box — not from VS Code or Cursor.
